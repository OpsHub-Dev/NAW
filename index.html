How It Works
The order flow

From browsing to finance — every step tracked.

1
Browse Market
Branch browses inventory, filters by vendor or storage category
2
Build Basket
Add items to basket, set quantities, review split by CWH / Vendor
3
Send Orders
CWH orders go to system. Vendor orders open Outlook with PDF email
4
Receive Items
Mark each item received, not received, or adjust quantity
5
Finance
Submit to Finance dashboard with full audit of ordered vs received
